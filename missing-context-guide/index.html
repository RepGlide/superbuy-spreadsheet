Missing Context Guide

When price, image, option, stock, or promotion lack their conditions, find what to re-check and where to pause before the balance tips.

Absence has weight

A page rarely announces what it is not showing. The missing conditions are the quiet side of the balance — and they tip it just as surely as a loud claim.

Price without conditions

If a price shows no currency, no quantity, and no option, you cannot tell what it covers. Re-check all three before acting on it.

Image without context

An image shows one photographed version. It does not show the option, the size, or the item you will receive. Re-check which version the image belongs to.

Option without a name

If a color, size, or version is not named, the option is unnamed. Re-check the selector to see which choice is actually selected.

Stock without a state

A listing can appear without telling you whether the item is available. Re-check the availability state on the external page rather than assuming.

Promotion without limits

A promotion can appear without its restrictions, exclusions, or expiry. Re-check what the claim applies to before relying on it.

Where to pause

Each absent condition is a notch. When the notches outweigh what the page actually shows, pause at the decision boundary and record what is missing — do not balance by assumption.
