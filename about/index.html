About

Last updated: September 4, 2026

What this resource is

Superbuy Spreadsheet is an independent informational and navigation resource for people who use a shared Superbuy spreadsheet. It links to product categories and to a fixed Google Sheet, and it explains a context balance — a way to separate directly visible details, derived notes, missing context, and decision boundaries so a loud claim does not outshout the context it is missing.

What this resource is not

This website is not the official Superbuy site, a store, a product database, a QC certification, a price comparator, a shopping agent, an order tracker, a fee calculator, or a purchase guarantee. It does not score products, compare prices, or auto-calculate a balance.

Independence

This resource is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Superbuy or Google. Any reference to Superbuy is descriptive, used to identify the spreadsheet topic this guide discusses.

No score or verification

The context balance is a recording routine, not a rating. It separates what is visible from what is missing, and it does not claim that either side can be weighed automatically.

How to use it

Open the sheet, browse a category, and record each read as directly visible, derived, missing, or a decision boundary. Pause before a claim outweighs its context.
